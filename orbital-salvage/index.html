HOW TO PLAY

Pick up.
Fly home.

01 / FLY

Point and go.

W / ↑ thrusts. A D / ← → turn. Release thrust to coast. The dotted line shows your drift.

02 / BRAKE

Use counter-thrust.

S / ↓ applies gentle reverse thrust along your nose; it can speed you up if you face against your drift. To brake, face against your drift and use W / ↑.

03 / PICK UP

Bring it aboard.

Get close and press SPACE. Cargo locks onto your ship and moves with it. Press again to drop it.

04 / DELIVER

Follow the home marker.

Fly into the marked home circle, also shown on the map. Cargo unloads automatically below 200 m/s on easy contracts, or 120 m/s on later ones. Check your speed and dock indicator.

P / ESC pauses · R restarts · M mutes.
