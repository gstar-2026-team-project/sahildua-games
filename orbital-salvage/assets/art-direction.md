# Orbital Salvage backdrop

`space-backdrop.png` is a locally bundled environment painting generated using
the built-in image generation tool. It is a static game asset, not a live API
dependency. Foreground ships, cargo, hazards, station and effects remain Canvas
artwork in `src/art.js` and `src/renderer.js`.

The painting moves slightly behind independent parallax stars. Its open central
area keeps physical objects readable. Colors contrast cool violet/blue space
with warm ivory/orange hardware and mint recovery effects.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide background painting for Orbital
Salvage, a top-down 2D browser space arcade game. Create a beautiful premium indie
game cosmic backdrop, panoramic 16:9 landscape. A monumental softly lit
turquoise-blue gas giant curves into the upper right, its delicate thin rings
crossing diagonally behind it. Vast luminous violet and electric-blue nebula
clouds curl around the outer edges, warm amber starlight behind distant dust at
lower left, tiny faraway stars. Rich layered painterly astronomical detail,
atmospheric depth, colorful and cinematic yet tasteful. Keep the central and
lower-middle 60 percent mostly open deep indigo-blue space so small bright
spacecraft and cargo remain easy to read. Fine wispy nebulosity, smooth light,
sophisticated saturated teal versus violet versus sunset-orange palette. This
is a pure environment painting, not a mockup. No spacecraft, no space station,
no asteroids, no text, no icons, no grids, no HUD, no watermark, no borders.
